    What goes wrong when nobody is watching?

    An online test only measures what students know if the test is the only thing on their screen.

    1. A second tab with the notes open. nothing notices
    2. Copy the question, paste it into a search box or a chatbot. nothing notices
    3. One paper for everyone, so answers travel through the group chat. same order for all
    4. The time limit is on the honour system. no real clock
    5. Then someone marks all of it by hand. your evening
